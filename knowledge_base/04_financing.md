## What You Need to Apply

1. Valid driver's license
2. Proof of income (recent pay stubs or tax returns)
3. Proof of residence (utility bill or lease agreement)
4. Social Security number (for credit check)
5. References (2 personal, 1 professional)
6. Down payment (varies by credit profile)

## Lease vs. Buy — Which Is Right for You?

### Lease if you:
- Want lower monthly payments
- Like driving a new car every 2–3 years
- Drive fewer than 15,000 miles per year
- Prefer to keep your car under factory warranty

### Buy if you:
- Plan to keep the car for 5+ years
- Drive more than 15,000 miles per year
- Want to build equity in a vehicle
- Customize or modify your vehicles
- Have no monthly payment after the loan ends

## Gap Insurance — $799
Covers the difference between what you owe and the car's actual cash value in the
event of a total loss. Required for financed and leased vehicles with less than
20% down.
