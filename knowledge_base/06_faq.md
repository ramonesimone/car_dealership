# Frequently Asked Questions — T&C AUTOS

## General

### Q: Where is T&C AUTOS located?
A: We are located at 4200 Stevens Creek Blvd, San Jose, CA 95129, in the heart of Silicon Valley.

### Q: What are your business hours?
A: Monday–Friday 9 AM–8 PM, Saturday 9 AM–7 PM, Sunday 10 AM–5 PM.

### Q: Do I need an appointment to visit the showroom?
A: No, walk-ins are always welcome. However, scheduling a test drive in advance
ensures the vehicle is ready and waiting for you.

### Q: Do you speak languages other than English?
A: Yes! Our team is multilingual and can assist you in English, Spanish, Mandarin,
Tagalog, and Hindi.

## Vehicles & Inventory

### Q: What types of vehicles do you sell?
A: We offer new, certified pre-owned (CPO), and used vehicles from 12 major
manufacturers including Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, BMW, Mercedes-Benz,
Tesla, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Subaru, and Volkswagen.

### Q: Do you have electric vehicles available?
A: Yes, we carry a growing inventory of EVs including Tesla Model 3, Model Y,
Kia EV6, Hyundai Ioniq, Volkswagen ID.4, and more. Our service center is also
EV-certified.

### Q: Can I see vehicle history reports?
A: Absolutely. Every used and CPO vehicle comes with a complimentary Carfax or
AutoCheck vehicle history report. Just ask any sales associate.

### Q: Are all vehicles inspected before sale?
A: Yes. Every vehicle undergoes a comprehensive inspection before being listed.
CPO vehicles go through a rigorous 150-point certification process.

## Test Drives

### Q: Can I test drive a vehicle?
A: Yes, test drives are encouraged. Bring a valid driver's license. We offer
standard test drives and extended overnight test drives on select vehicles
(with approved credit).

### Q: Do I need an appointment for a test drive?
A: Not required, but recommended so we can have the vehicle prepared and warmed
up for you.

### Q: Can I take a vehicle for an independent inspection?
A: Yes. We welcome independent inspections. You may take the vehicle to a
mechanic of your choice within 5 miles. A 24-hour window is provided for this purpose.

## Financing

### Q: What credit score do I need to finance a car?
A: We work with buyers across the credit spectrum. Excellent rates are available
for 680+, but we have programs starting at 580 credit score through our special
financing partners.

### Q: Do you offer first-time buyer programs?
A: Yes, we have a First-Time Buyer program with rates starting at 5.99% APR and
flexible terms for buyers with limited credit history.

### Q: What documents do I need to apply for financing?
A: Valid driver's license, proof of income (pay stubs or tax returns), proof of
residence, Social Security number, and references.

### Q: How long does financing approval take?
A: Pre-approval typically takes 2 hours or less. Same-day approval is available
for most applicants.

### Q: Is there a penalty for paying off my loan early?
A: No. T&C AUTOS financing has no prepayment penalties. You can pay off your loan
at any time with no additional fees.

## Service

### Q: Do I need an appointment for service?
A: Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments receive priority and guaranteed
same-day service for most maintenance if you arrive by 10 AM.

### Q: How long does an oil change take?
A: An oil change typically takes 30–45 minutes. No appointment needed for this
service.

### Q: Do you offer loaner vehicles?
A: Yes, complimentary loaner vehicles are available with 48-hour advance booking.
Free shuttle service is also available within 5 miles.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept for service?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, cash, and certified
checks.

### Q: Is there a warranty on service work?
A: Yes. All repairs come with a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor.

## Purchasing

### Q: Can I buy a car online?
A: Yes. We offer a full online purchase experience. Browse inventory, apply for
financing, complete paperwork, and arrange delivery or pickup — all from home.

### Q: Do you offer delivery?
A: Yes, we offer local delivery within 50 miles of our San Jose location.
Nationwide shipping is also available at cost.

### Q: What is your price match guarantee?
A: If you find the same vehicle for less at a local dealership within 100 miles,
we'll match the price and give you an additional $200 off.

### Q: Can I return a car after purchase?
A: We offer a 7-day / 500-mile exchange policy. Used vehicle buyers also have a
3-business-day cancellation option (subject to a $250 restocking fee).

## Trade-Ins

### Q: How does the trade-in process work?
A: Bring your vehicle for a free 30-minute appraisal. We provide a written offer
valid for 7 days. Trade-in value reduces the taxable amount on your new purchase.

### Q: Do I have to buy a car to trade mine in?
A: No. We'll buy your vehicle outright even if you don't purchase from us.

## General

### Q: Is T&C AUTOS an authorized dealer for all the brands you sell?
A: We are a multi-brand dealership. While we are an authorized dealer for select
manufacturers, we also source low-mileage CPO and used vehicles from all major
brands to provide the widest selection.

### Q: Do you offer extended warranties?
A: Yes. We offer three tiers: Powertrain Plus (5yr/60K), Platinum Shield
(7yr/100K bumper-to-bumper), and Ultimate Care (7yr/100K with maintenance included).

### Q: Are there any hidden fees?
A: No. We believe in transparent pricing. All fees are disclosed upfront. Your
out-the-door price includes vehicle price, tax, title, license, and a $85
documentation fee.
