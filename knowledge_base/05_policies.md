# T&C AUTOS Policies

## Sales Policies

### Price Match Guarantee
If you find a lower advertised price on the same vehicle (same year, make, model,
trim, and condition) at a licensed dealership within 100 miles, we'll match the
price and give you an additional $200 off. Must present the competing offer in
writing within 7 days of purchase or before delivery.

### 7-Day Exchange Policy
If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may exchange the vehicle
for another of equal or greater value within 7 days and 500 miles. Vehicle must be
in the same condition as sold, with no damage or modifications.

### 3-Day Cancellation (Used Vehicles Only)
For used vehicles, you may cancel the sale within 3 business days and return the
vehicle for a full refund minus a $250 restocking fee. Mileage cannot exceed 150
miles from the time of delivery.

## Warranty Information

### New Vehicles
Every new vehicle comes with the manufacturer's factory warranty:
- **Bumper-to-Bumper:** 3 years / 36,000 miles (whichever comes first)
- **Powertrain:** 5 years / 60,000 miles
- **Corrosion:** 5 years / unlimited miles
- **Emissions:** 7 years / 70,000 miles (California)

### Certified Pre-Owned Vehicles
Each CPO vehicle includes:
- 150-point inspection certification
- Remaining factory warranty plus 1 year / 12,000 miles CPO add-on
- 24/7 roadside assistance
- Trip interruption coverage

### Used Vehicles
Sold "as-is" unless an extended warranty is purchased. All used vehicles come with
a free 30-day / 1,000-mile limited powertrain warranty.

## Service Policies

### Warranty on Repairs
- 12 months / 12,000 miles on all parts and labor
- OEM parts warranty as specified by manufacturer
- All work guaranteed — if a issue reoccurs, we fix it free

### Appointment Policy
- Walk-ins welcome, but appointments receive priority
- Same-day service available for most maintenance (arrive by 10 AM)
- Free shuttle service within 5 miles
- Loaner vehicles available with 48-hour advance booking

### Payment Methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, cash, certified check, and T&C
AUTOS credit card.

## Trade-In Policy

1. Bring your vehicle for a free appraisal (30 minutes)
2. We provide a written offer valid for 7 days
3. Offer is good whether you purchase or not
4. Trade-in value reduces the taxable amount on your new vehicle
5. Payoff of existing loans handled directly by our finance team

## Privacy Policy

T&C AUTOS respects your privacy. We do not sell or share your personal information
with third parties for marketing purposes. See our full privacy policy at
tcautos.com/privacy.

## Accessibility

T&C AUTOS is committed to providing an accessible experience. Our showroom and
service center are fully ADA-compliant. We offer:
- Wheelchair-accessible entrance and restrooms
- Sign language interpretation available with 48-hour notice
- Large-print documents available on request
